Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 12 2026

MonoWeb ("we", "us", or "our") operates monoweb.com.au. This page explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website or services.

Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (if provided)
  • Business name
  • Any message or project details you share with us

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiries about our services
  • Provide quotes and discuss project requirements
  • Deliver website design and development services
  • Communicate with you about your project

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How We Store Your Information

Information submitted through our contact form is delivered to us via email and stored securely. Your website files are hosted on secure servers. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. Project-related information is kept for business records and may be retained for up to 7 years for tax and legal compliance purposes.

Your Rights

Under Australian privacy law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of your personal information
  • Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal requirements)

Cookies and Tracking

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymous information such as the number of visitors, pages viewed, and traffic sources. This helps us improve our website and services.

The information collected by Google Analytics is processed in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy, available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
