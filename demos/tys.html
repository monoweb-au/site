What is Knoff Yoga?

Knoff Yoga is a traditional style of Hatha Yoga, which utilises the body as a vehicle/tool to increase awareness. All of the techniques—meditation, breath-work, postures, relaxation and philosophy—are designed to turn the mind inwards.

The idea is to be in the present moment, not thinking about the past or projecting into the future. Increased awareness, combined with increased mobility and vitality, gives you the opportunity to make the most of your precious life.

The goal of Knoff Yoga is to give you the tools and encouragement to make positive changes in the direction you choose. Life has provided you with a vehicle. Knoff Yoga is the owner's manual.

Our Programs

4 Levels of Study

Comprehensive yoga education for students of all experience levels

Level 1 - Foundation

Perfect for beginners. Learn the fundamentals of Knoff Yoga including proper alignment, breathing techniques, and basic postures in a supportive environment.

Level 2 - Development

Build upon your foundation with more advanced postures and extended holds. Develop strength, flexibility, and deepen your understanding of yoga philosophy.

Level 3 - Advanced Practice

Refine your technique with challenging asanas. Focus on subtle body awareness, advanced breathing techniques, and deeper meditation practices.

Level 4 - Mastery

Intensive practice for dedicated students. Integrate all aspects of yoga philosophy and practice. Preparation for teacher training available.
