Which Driver Are You?

Defensive driving goes beyond following the road rules — it's about anticipating hazards, staying in control, and reducing risk.

  Reactionary Anticipatory
Focus Waits until a hazard is already present before responding Thinks ahead, scans the road, and predicts what other drivers might do
Behaviour Sudden braking, swerving, or erratic corrections at the last moment Smooth, calm, and in control. Adjusts early to avoid problems
Mindset Tense, anxious, or frustrated. Things "happen to them" Calm and confident. Feels in charge because they are prepared
We help you become an anticipatory driver
