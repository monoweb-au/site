Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services.

How many sections do I get on one page?

+

Starter includes 4 sections, Plus includes 6. Common sections include Hero, About, Services, Testimonials, Gallery, FAQ, and Contact. Extra sections can be added for $100 each.

What types of businesses is this service best suited for?

+

Anyone who needs a quality online presence — trades, cafés, consultants, creatives, and professionals of all kinds. If you need a no-fuss site that works, this is built for you.

Will this work if I have a big menu or lots of updates?

+

Our single-page sites are designed to present information clearly. They work best for businesses that can group or summarise their services, rather than listing large menus, detailed lists, or content that changes frequently.


If your business requires a full menu, extensive listings, or regular content updates, a different type of website setup may be a better fit.

How fast can you build it once I send my details?

+

Most websites are completed within 7–14 days from when we start. The faster you can get us your content and feedback, the quicker we'll have you live. We'll keep you updated the whole way.

What do you need from me to start?

+

Just your business details, logo, and any photos you've got. Don't stress if you're not sure what works – we'll guide you through it.

What if I'm not happy with the design?

+

We'll work with you from day one to make sure we're on the same page. Your revision rounds are there to tweak things until it feels right. Need more changes beyond that? We'll chat about it and sort you out.

Do I own my domain and website files?

+

Yep! It's your domain, your files, your website. If you ever want to move it somewhere else or manage it yourself, we'll hand everything over – no dramas.

Can I add extras to my package?

+

Yes — optional extras like additional sections, stock photos, or extra revision rounds can be added to any package. Everything is quoted upfront before any work begins.

What are the ongoing costs after launch?

+

Hosting is $29/month and covers keeping your site live, secure, and supported. You'll also need to renew your domain annually (typically $15–$20) — that's registered in your name and we handle all the technical setup.

How do changes and updates work once the site is live?

+

Small edits like address, email and phone number updates are included with your hosting plan. Beyond that, any changes are quoted as needed. Some clients never need updates at all, others like to tweak things here and there. Completely up to you – no pressure either way.

Can I add extra sections or features later down the track?

+

Absolutely. Simple additions like extra sections, pages, image galleries, booking links, or contact updates can be added later and are quoted based on scope.

More complex features (such as blogs, online stores, membership accounts, and custom systems) are outside the scope of our single-page sites and are usually better suited to a different type of platform.
