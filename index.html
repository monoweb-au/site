Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services.

How many sections do I get on one page?

+

Each website is built as a single-page layout. The Starter package includes 4 sections and the Plus package includes 6 sections.


Common sections include: Hero, About, Services, Testimonials, Pricing, FAQ, and Contact. This approach keeps the website fast, simple, and easy for visitors to understand. If you need additional sections, they can be purchased as an extra.

What types of businesses is this service best suited for?

+

This service works best for local service-based businesses such as trades, studios, consultants, professionals, cafés, and small businesses that want a clear, professional online presence without ongoing complexity.

Will this work if I have a big menu or lots of updates?

+

Our one-page websites are designed to present information clearly and simply. They work best for businesses that can group or summarise their services, rather than listing large menus, detailed price lists, or content that changes frequently.

If your business requires a full menu, extensive listings, or regular content updates, a different type of website setup may be a better fit.

How fast can you build it once I send my details?

+

Most websites are completed within 7-14 days from when we start. The faster you can get us your content and feedback, the quicker we'll have you live. We'll keep you updated the whole way.

What do you need from me to start?

+

Just your business details, logo, and any photos you've got. Don't stress if you're not sure what works – we'll guide you through it.

What if I'm not happy with the design?

+

We'll work with you from day one to make sure we're on the same page. Your revision rounds are there to tweak things until it feels right. Need more changes beyond that? We'll chat about it and sort you out.

Do I own my domain and website files?

+

Yep! It's your domain, your files, your website. If you ever want to move it somewhere else or manage it yourself, we'll hand everything over – no dramas.

Can I add extras to my package?

+

Your package price is fixed and covers everything listed in your chosen tier. However, some businesses choose to add optional extras like stock image sourcing, professional image optimization, additional sections beyond your package, or extra revision rounds.

These are completely optional and quoted upfront before any work begins. There are no surprise charges – if something isn't included in your package, we'll let you know and give you the option to add it or proceed without it.

What are the ongoing costs after launch?

+

There's a $20AUD/month hosting fee that covers keeping your site online, SSL certificates, DNS management, and technical support if anything goes wrong. You can cancel anytime and move your hosting elsewhere – no lock-in.


Like all websites, you'll need to renew your domain each year (usually $15–$20). That's standard whether you're with us, Wix, Squarespace, or anyone else. We'll walk you through setting it up – it's pretty straightforward.


Design changes or content updates are completely optional and quoted separately – no obligation for ongoing work.

How do changes and updates work once the site is live?

+

We'll happily make small edits for free within 30 days of launch. After that, any changes are quoted as needed. Some clients never need updates at all, others like to tweak things here and there. Completely up to you – no pressure either way.

Can I add extra sections or features later down the track?

+

Absolutely. Simple additions like extra sections, pages, image galleries, booking links, or contact updates can be added later and are quoted based on scope.

More complex features (such as blogs, online stores, membership accounts, and custom systems) are outside the scope of our one-page websites and are usually better suited to a different type of platform.
